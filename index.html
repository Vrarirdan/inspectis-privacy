Privacy Policy for Inspectis

Effective Date: January 18, 2026

Inspectis ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use the Inspectis application.

Information We Collect

Inspectis collects only the information necessary to provide its core functionality. This may include:

  • Inspection and project data entered by the user
  • Photos, notes, and annotations created during inspections
  • Optional profile information such as company name, inspector name, email address, and phone number

Inspectis does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

How Information Is Used

Information collected by Inspectis is used solely to create and manage inspection projects, generate reports and exports, and display inspection data within the app. No data is used for advertising, analytics tracking, or marketing purposes.

Data Storage

Inspection data is stored locally on the user's device and/or in services explicitly configured by the user. Inspectis does not automatically transmit or store user data on external servers unless initiated by the user.

Permissions

Inspectis may request access to the following device features:

  • Camera - to capture inspection photos
  • Storage - to save and export inspection files

These permissions are used only to support core app functionality.

Data Sharing

Inspectis does not share user data with third parties except when required to complete user-initiated actions such as exporting or sharing reports.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect user data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

Children's Privacy

Inspectis is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
